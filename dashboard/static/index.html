LOCAL-LAN INFERENCE RESEARCH PROTOTYPE

EdgeSplit

Split LLM inference across the hardware you already own.

LLMs have two distinct phases: prefill (context processing) and decode (token generation). EdgeSplit splits the work, prefill runs on your laptop GPU, decode runs on your Android phone over Wi-Fi.

Prefill needs your laptop's GPU, decode doesn't. EdgeSplit frees your laptop the moment prefill finishes, so its compute is available for other work while your phone handles decode.

Laptop Prefill

Fast context processing on your GPU.

Wi-Fi State Transfer

Efficient state handoff over your local LAN.

Phone Decode

Token generation on your Android device.

ENGINEERING CHALLENGES
Scope and limitations

        
An Android source-build compatibility limitation is documented in the README’s engineering notes. It does not affect the demonstrated laptop-to-phone LAN path.
A fixed-seed, temperature-zero 64-token correctness gate produced identical generated text from stock and optimized binaries after excluding llama-cli's timing footer.
Phone battery power is intentionally absent from these tables. The initial current_avg vendor fuel-gauge sysfs source was invalid: filtering rejected 162/176 samples in a plugged window and 179/180 in an unplugged window, with values as high as 40 A and 251.406 A.

      


      

        
SUMMARY
EdgeSplit

        
V2 is additive: it does not replace V1, delete V1 artifacts, or alter the stock /slots save/restore routes.

        
This is a single-request experimental system, not a production distributed inference service.

        
Prefix caching remains optional follow-up work. The NEON kernel is completed as a separately scoped Phone stretch result and is not part of the V1/V2 architectural claim.
